How this works in the museum

Place a QR code near each exhibit point in your 360 museum. A visitor scans the code, lands on a dedicated viewer page, rotates the model in-browser, and can use the AR option on supported devices.

Note: Android devices are the most reliable for direct GLB-to-AR flows. iPhone users will still get the web viewer, but full native Quick Look AR usually needs a USDZ version of the same model.
